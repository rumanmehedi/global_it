About our company

Global IT Technology is an Australian family-owned business founded in Melbourne.

Our founder and director brings to the table 20 years' experience working in telecommunications and Technology as a business analyst, project manager and system administrator across different industries.

Global IT Technology was founded to follow her dream of establishing an Australia-based company that, different from other IT companies, could offer flexible services and count on a highly skilled team.

Based on her experience of seeing clients under pressure to purchase overpriced items that they do not necessarily need, Global IT Technology's mission is to create a trustworthy relationship with customers.
